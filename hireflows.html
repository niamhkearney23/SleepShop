How We Work

A good hire versus a great hire. A CV that looks right versus a candidate who is right. Us versus every other legal recruiter. The difference is always context.

We thirst to understand

Your firm. Your practice. Your partners. Your culture. The matter you're staffing. The client relationship behind it. We don't just listen — we go deeper. It's the only way we can succeed.

We're a tech company that doesn't talk about tech

The best technology disappears. Our custom software helps our team source faster, screen better, and understand context deeper. You'll never see it. What you'll see is the result.

Every candidate is scored on ten dimensions

Critical thinking, written communication, judgement, attitude, attention to detail, resilience, and more. Every applicant. Same standard. Fully auditable.

We're built for law firms who care

Boutique litigators. Mid-market corporate. In-house teams. If you care who represents your firm to candidates as much as we do, we're the right partner.

Common Questions

Everything you'd want to ask.

What types of legal roles do you recruit for?

Paralegals, associates, senior associates, partners, in-house counsel, legal operations, compliance, and firm leadership. We recruit across litigation, corporate, IP, employment, real estate, tax, and regulatory practice areas.

How long does a typical search take?

Paralegal roles: 2–4 weeks from kickoff to signed offer. Associate roles: 4–8 weeks. Partner and senior in-house roles: 8–16 weeks. We prioritise fit over speed, but we won't sit on searches.

How does pricing work?

Contingent placements for paralegal and junior roles. Retained search for senior associates, partners, and leadership positions. We're transparent about fees on the first call — no games.

What makes you different from other legal recruiters?

We built AI-native infrastructure so our team can spend their time on judgement, not admin. Every candidate is vetted against a structured rubric. Every conversation is contextualised. We only send candidates worth your partners' time.

How do you vet candidates?

Every applicant completes a structured assessment scored across ten dimensions — critical thinking, written communication, judgement, attitude, attention to detail, and more. A recruiter then interviews shortlisted candidates before they reach you.

What role does AI play in your process?

AI handles the first-pass screening, resume analysis, and structured scoring. Our people make every judgement call that matters. You'll never receive a candidate we haven't personally spoken to.

Do you work with boutique firms as well as large firms?

Yes. We work with sole practices, boutique litigation firms, mid-market corporate firms, and in-house legal departments. What matters is that you value quality candidates and treat your brand carefully.

Do you offer any guarantees?

Yes. If a placed candidate leaves within the first 90 days for reasons unrelated to firm changes, we replace them at no additional fee. We'd rather never need to.
