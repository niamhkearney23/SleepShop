The Promise

They send noise.
We send signal.

Most legal recruiters send volume and hope something sticks. We don't. Every candidate is vetted, screened, and contextualised. If we're not confident it's worth your partners' time, we don't hit send.

01

We only bring you conversations worth having

Your billable hours matter. If a candidate isn't ready to sit across from you, we don't send them. Full stop.

02

We work fast, but we don't rush

Great hires take time. We move as quickly as we can without cutting corners, because a bad hire costs a firm more than a slow one.

03

We take the long view

We assume we'll be working with you for years. We won't push a weak candidate to close a fee, and we'll tell you to wait on a hire even when it costs us.

04

We push back

When a job spec is too broad, we'll say so. When the comp doesn't match the market, we'll say so. Partners, not order-takers.

05

Structured, not subjective

Every candidate is assessed against the same rubric. Same standard. Same dimensions. Fully auditable for every partner in the room.

06

We treat your brand as our own

Every candidate's first impression of your firm often comes from us. Every email, every call, every interaction matters. Your reputation is our reputation.

07

AI does the sifting, humans do the judgement

The system handles the sifting, sourcing, and follow-up. People spend that time where it actually counts — on judgement.

08

We will find your dream candidate

Not might. Not try. Will. Sometimes it takes longer than expected. Sometimes we have to look in unexpected places. We don't stop until we get it right.

How It Works

Context is everything.

A good hire versus a great hire. A CV that looks right versus a candidate who is right. The difference is always context — and the system is designed around that.

We start by understanding

Your firm. Your practice. Your partners. Your culture. The role you're filling and the person you actually need. We don't send anyone until we know all of that.

Technology that disappears

The system handles the sifting, sourcing, scoring, and follow-up. What you see is the result: a shortlist worth your time and a decision within a day.

Structured scoring on every applicant

Critical thinking, written communication, judgement, attitude, attention to detail, resilience, and more. Ten dimensions. Same standard. Every candidate. Fully auditable.

Built for firms who care

Boutique litigators. Mid-market corporate. In-house teams. If you care who represents your firm to candidates as much as we do, we're the right partner.

Are we the right fit?

We don't work with everyone.

We work best with firms who want a partner across every hire — that's where cohesion, context, and speed drive unmatched results.

We're built for

  • Firms who value quality over volume
  • Managing partners who want a trusted advisor
  • Firms building for the next decade, not the next quarter
  • Teams who treat their brand as sacred
  • Boutique and mid-market firms with distinctive cultures

We're not for

  • Firms looking for the cheapest option
  • Anyone who wants fast over good
  • Partners who need order-takers
  • Firms treating candidates as inventory
  • Anyone unwilling to invest in getting hiring right
Common Questions

Everything you'd want to ask.

What types of legal roles is Hireflows built for?

Paralegals to start. The same approach extends to associates, senior associates, in-house counsel, and legal operations roles. Book a call to discuss the role you're hiring for.

How does the assessment work?

Every applicant completes a short structured assessment. The system scores their answers across ten dimensions — critical thinking, written communication, judgement, attitude, attention to detail, and more — and produces a total score with a written rationale.

How fast do candidates hear back?

Within 24 hours of submitting the assessment. A shortlisted candidate can be on a screening call the same week.

Who makes the final decision?

You do. The AI produces a first-pass score; a person at your firm has a 24-hour window to override every decision before any email goes out. Every candidate can be accepted or rejected with a single click.

How does pricing work?

Get in touch and we'll walk you through it. We keep it simple and transparent — no games.

What role does AI play?

AI handles the first-pass screening, resume analysis, and structured scoring. People at your firm make every judgement call that matters. Nothing is auto-sent for 24 hours.

Where does the data live?

Inside your own Google Workspace. The only content the AI reads is the answers to the assessment questions.
