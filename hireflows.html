Why Hireflows

Every candidate gets a fair read. Every partner gets their time back.

A quiet, deliberate system that handles the parts of hiring that shouldn't need a partner in the loop.

01

Structured, not subjective

Every applicant is scored against the same ten dimensions — critical thinking, judgement, written communication, attention to detail, and more. Same rubric, every time.

02

Human veto, always

Nothing goes out for 24 hours. Every acceptance or decline can be overridden with a single cell change. The AI does the reading; the firm does the deciding.

03

Inside your workspace

The whole pipeline runs in your firm's own Google Sheet, Drive, and Gmail. Nothing leaves. No new dashboard to learn.

How it works

From application to signed offer, without the admin drag.

01   Apply

The candidate finds one link.

Shared on your website, LinkedIn, or auto-replied when anyone emails your firm about a paralegal role. The applicant answers eight questions and uploads a CV.

02   Assess

The system scores across ten dimensions.

Critical thinking. Written communication. Judgement. Attitude. Attention to detail. Resilience. Every applicant, same standard, written rationale included.

03   Review

You get 24 hours to override anything.

Every candidate lands in the sheet immediately. Emails are queued, not sent. Change a cell to accept, decline, delay, or send early.

04   Screen

Shortlisted candidates book themselves in.

Calendly link in their acceptance email. Zoom or Google Meet auto-generated. A 15-minute call slots into your week without any coordination.

05   Decide

Click Accept, and the offer paperwork begins.

Every detail — name, contact, scores, screening notes, resume — already in the sheet. One click, and the person handling offers gets exactly what they need.

The Hireflows Principle
Common questions

Everything you'd want to ask.

What types of legal roles is Hireflows built for?

Paralegals to start. The same approach extends to associates, senior associates, in-house counsel, and legal operations. Book a call to discuss the role you're hiring for.

How does the assessment work?

Every applicant completes a short structured assessment. Their answers are scored across ten dimensions — critical thinking, written communication, judgement, attitude, attention to detail, and more — producing a total score out of 100 with a written rationale.

How fast do candidates hear back?

Within 24 hours of submitting. A shortlisted candidate can be on a screening call the same week.

Who makes the final decision?

Your firm does. The AI produces a first-pass score; anyone with sheet access has 24 hours to override before any email goes out.

How does pricing work?

Get in touch and we'll walk you through it. Simple and transparent.

Where does the data live?

Inside your firm's own Google Workspace. The only content the AI reads is the answers to the assessment questions.
