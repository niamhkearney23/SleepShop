The problem today

Recruitment eats billable hours.

  • Every CV is read manually, often twice.
  • Hours are spent sifting inboxes and replying to applicants one by one.
  • No structured way to compare candidates on judgement or attitude.
  • Shortlisting, scheduling, and reply emails all live in separate places.
  • Candidates wait days — sometimes weeks — for a decision.
The solution

One link. One pipeline. One source of truth.

Applicants reach the online assessment in one of two ways:

Route 1 — QR code or link

Shared on the firm's website, LinkedIn posts, or direct outreach. Scan or click, and the assessment opens.

Route 2 — Auto-reply from email

Any email to the firm asking about paralegal work is answered automatically with the assessment link. No inbox chasing.

From: careers@thomasphilip.com.my  ·  Subject: Thank you for your interest in Thomas Philip

Dear Applicant,

Thank you for reaching out about paralegal opportunities at Thomas Philip Advocates & Solicitors.

FAQ shown alongside the form

How long does it take? Around 20 minutes.

When will I hear back? Within 24 hours of submitting.

Can I save and come back? Yes, your progress is stored in your browser.

Is this a real job? Yes — successful applicants are invited to a 15-minute screening call.

Who reads my answers? The firm. An AI produces the first score; a person makes the final call.

Inside the assessment

Ten quality scores. One recommendation.

The AI reads only the answers to the eight questions and rates the candidate on ten quality dimensions, then produces a total score out of 100 with a written rationale.

Critical Thinking

Logic, structure, and reasoning in written answers.

Written Communication

Clarity, tone, and professionalism.

Judgement

Handling of ambiguous scenarios.

Self-Awareness

Honest reflection on strengths and gaps.

Attitude & Motivation

Why law, why this firm, what drives them.

Innovation

Creative problem-solving, resourcefulness.

Attention to Detail

Spelling, grammar, factual precision.

Time Management

How they handle competing deadlines.

Resilience

Response to setbacks and feedback.

Plus Professional Integrity — scored separately.

The safety net

Nothing goes out for 24 hours.

  • Every submission lands in the Applications sheet immediately.
  • The candidate email is marked SCHEDULED — not sent.
  • Anyone with sheet access has a full day to simply override the AI's call.
  • To override: change one cell in a specific column. That's it.
  • To send early or delay: update the timestamp on the row.
  • Scores of 60 or above → acceptance email queued. Below 60 → rejection email queued.

Behind the scenes

One sheet. Everything connected.

  • Applications sheet — every submission, colour-coded by decision (green / amber / red).
  • Candidate report — formatted Google Doc auto-generated per submission with all answers, scores, and AI rationale.
  • Resume — uploaded PDF saved to the firm's Drive folder, renamed with the candidate's name, linked from the sheet.
  • Calendly — candidates self-book screening calls; Zoom or Google Meet link auto-generated.
  • Audit trail — every status change, decision, and email is timestamped in the sheet.

It looks like a lot at first, but everything leads back to the same Google Sheet.

Where this goes next

The future roadmap.

  • Automatic offer letter — pre-populated from sheet data and sent without manual drafting.
  • Resume summary — the AI reads the entire CV and produces a one-paragraph summary alongside the assessment score.
  • WhatsApp approval — a message pings a partner: "Candidate X scored 82. Accept or reject?" A reply overrides the AI in real time.
  • Booking follow-up — automatic nudge if a shortlisted candidate hasn't booked their screening call within three days.
  • Human veto, always — every one of these steps can be overridden with a single reply or a single cell change.

Risks & mitigations

What could go wrong — and why it won't.

24-hour review window; every decision can be overridden in one click.

Data privacy

All data stays inside the firm's own Google Workspace. The only part the AI reads is the answers to the eight questions.

System downtime

Form submissions retry automatically; sheet entries are atomic. Zero data loss in testing.
