The Purpose

This is your audition for pupillage.

At Thomas Philip, every pupillage candidate must first complete a paralegalship. It is not an internship. It is a three-month test of whether you can be trusted to represent this firm as a pupil.

For You

A structured entry into real litigation, with real cases, real courtrooms, and real feedback. You leave with skills — pupillage offer or not.

For Us

Time to see how you work — how you think, write, handle pressure, and treat the people around you. No CV shows that.

04 · Requirements

The Baseline

Who we're looking for.

The baseline is clear. Everything after that comes down to attitude.

  • An LL.B, or having sat your final exams (you don't need to have formally graduated)
  • Strong written and spoken English and Bahasa Malaysia
  • Integrity, diligence, initiative, humility, and consistency — not five values on a wall, five things we actually notice
  • Willingness to be in the office, on time, in professional dress, every day for three months
05 · The Work
06 / 14
Your Day

What you'll actually do.

You will report to a supervising lawyer and, depending on active matters, take work from several. Your core scope covers:

Drafting

Affidavits, writs, submissions, cause papers, and client correspondence — always reviewed, always feedback.

Legal Research

Using CLJ, Lexis Nexis and our internal precedent database. Real questions from real matters.

Court Bundles

Compiling, indexing, and preparing the documents that lawyers rely on in hearings.

Court Attendance

Observing hearings, assisting with filing, and supporting lawyers in live proceedings when opportunities arise.

08 · The Pathway

Application to Pupillage

Four steps in.

1

Complete the Assessment

Eight written questions under timed conditions. We assess how you think, write, and make decisions — not legal knowledge.

2

We'll Contact You

Once you've submitted, we'll be in touch about next steps — including an interview if you're shortlisted.

3

Three-Month Paralegalship

Hands-on work across civil and commercial litigation, dispute resolution and advisory work. Weekly supervision. Real feedback.

4

Pupillage Consideration

Decisions rest on performance, attitude, reliability, and available openings. You'll be told by the end of your second month.

12 · Common Questions

FAQ

Things people ask.

Do I need to have graduated?
No — once you've sat your final exams, you can apply.
Is the programme paid?
Yes — RM 2,000 per month, throughout.
Will I attend court?
Yes, when case schedules allow — for observation or assistance.
Who supervises me?
A supervising lawyer, plus tasks from others depending on active matters.
Can I choose my department?
No — assignments are based on workload and firm needs.
Can I work remotely?
No. Office attendance is required throughout.
Is pupillage guaranteed?
No. It depends on performance and available openings.
When will I know about pupillage?
By the end of your second month.
