Inside the assessment

Eight questions. Plus an FAQ.

  • About the candidate — hobbies, interests, spare time.
  • A situation when something went wrong and how they handled it.
  • How they would triage three urgent tasks arriving at once.
  • A Friday 4:45 PM scenario — pressure and judgement.
  • What they think a paralegal actually does day-to-day.
  • Rewriting a poorly-written client email.
  • A client wants to sue — a critical-thinking scenario.
  • Anything else they want the firm to know.

A built-in FAQ answers common candidate questions before they submit — what happens next, how long it takes, whether they can save and return.

How the AI works

Every answer, scored consistently.

The AI reads only the eight answers the candidate submits. Nothing else. It scores them on:

  • Critical Thinking
  • Written Communication
  • Judgement
  • Self-Awareness
  • Attitude & Motivation
  • Innovation
  • Attention to Detail
  • Time Management
  • Resilience
  • Professional Integrity

Total out of 100

Below 60 Automatic rejection email.

Above 60 Automatic acceptance with a booking link for a screening call.

Every score is visible in the Google Sheet, along with a written rationale — strengths, concerns, and recommendation.

The safety net

Nothing sends for 24 hours.

  • Every submission lands in the Google Sheet immediately.
  • The candidate's email is queued as SCHEDULED — not yet sent.
  • We have a full day to override any decision.
  • To override: type a single word in one column of the sheet.
  • To send earlier: change the scheduled time to now.
  • To send immediately: run the queue with one click.

In practice

The AI is right most of the time. The 24-hour window means a human always has the final say. No embarrassing auto-emails, no rushed decisions.

Data privacy

Everything stays in the firm's Google Workspace.

  • All submissions, resumes, and reports live inside the firm's own Google Sheets and Drive.
  • The only information the AI ever reads is the candidate's answers to the eight questions.
  • The AI does not see the resume, the IC number, the address, or the phone.
  • No third-party database, no external CRM, no data leaves the firm's Google Workspace.

One source of truth

It looks like a lot of moving parts, but every route — assessment, review, email, interview, offer — leads back to the same Google Sheet. If it's not in the sheet, it didn't happen.
