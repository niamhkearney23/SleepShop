Paralegal Application

Around 20 minutes. Your answers are read carefully by a person after an initial AI score. You'll hear back within 24 hours.

1. Tell us about yourself and why you are drawn to law.
A few sentences on your background and what pulled you toward legal work.
2. Describe a time you handled a difficult deadline.
What was the situation, what did you do, and what was the outcome?
3. You spot a mistake in a partner's draft. What do you do?
Show us how you approach a delicate professional situation.
4. Summarise a legal news story you have read recently.
A short paragraph in your own words. Malaysian or international, either works.
5. Which of your qualities will make you a strong paralegal?
6. Describe a mistake you have made and what you learnt.
7. Write a short client email declining a meeting request.
Write the actual email you would send. No template needed.
