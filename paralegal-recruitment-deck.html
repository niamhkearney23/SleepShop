Candidate experience

What they do.

  • Open the link — from the website, a QR code, or the auto-reply email.
  • Answer 8 structured questions — about themselves, judgement scenarios, and short written exercises.
  • Upload a CV — stored securely in the firm's Drive.
  • Get instant confirmation — "We've received your application."
  • Within 24 hours — a personalised decision email arrives.

Three outcomes

Accept Invite to 15-min Zoom screening call via Calendly.

Borderline Sent for a full human review before a decision.

Reject Polite decline, encouragement to reapply.

Inside the assessment

Claude scores their answers.

Every answer is read by Claude and scored on ten quality dimensions. The scores add up to a total out of 100, with a written rationale we can read back in the sheet.

Critical Thinking

Logic, structure, and reasoning in written answers.

Written Communication

Clarity, tone, and professionalism.

Judgement

Handling of ambiguous, high-pressure scenarios.

Self-Awareness

Honest reflection on strengths and gaps.

Attitude & Motivation

Why law, why this firm, what drives them.

Innovation

Creative problem-solving, resourcefulness.

Attention to Detail

Spelling, grammar, factual precision.

Time Management

How they handle competing deadlines.

Resilience

Response to setbacks and feedback.

Plus Professional Integrity — scored separately. Below 60 → rejection email. Above 60 → acceptance email.

The safety net

Nothing goes out for 24 hours.

  • Every submission lands in the Applications sheet immediately.
  • The candidate email is queued — not sent.
  • Anyone at the firm has a full day to simply override the AI's call.
  • To override: type into the decision column in the Google Sheet.
  • To send early: update the timestamp; to send now, tick the "send immediately" box.

Why this matters

AI gets most decisions right. The 24-hour window means the firm stays in control of every single rejection and every single offer.

Behind the scenes

One sheet. Everything connected.

  • Applications sheet — every submission, colour-coded by decision (green / amber / red).
  • Candidate report — auto-generated Google Doc with all answers, scores, and Claude's rationale.
  • Resume — uploaded PDF saved to Drive, renamed with the candidate's name, linked from the sheet.
  • Calendly — candidates self-book screening calls; Zoom link auto-generated.
  • Audit trail — every status change, decision, and email is timestamped.
